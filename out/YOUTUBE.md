## Description

```
A Gregorian chant for HALO-90, the open-hardware earring with ninety charlieplexed LEDs, sung in Latin about how it works.

Everything on screen comes from the design files: KiCad's 3D export of the board with every copper trace under the soldermask, the STEP earwire and magnetic case, and the firmware's own LED patterns, run the way the device runs them. The +13 Halo scan speeds up to the chip's own 6.3 kHz until the ring glows evenly; audio mode listens to this very song through the mic and ADC; sparkle lights a random LED on one wake-up in fifteen; and a 500 ms hold puts it to sleep at 15 µA.
```
